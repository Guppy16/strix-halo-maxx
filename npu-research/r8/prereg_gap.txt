Hypotheses for the 37 cyc/call:
 H1 in-call memory stalls in mm_core (denser L1 traffic: ~290 accesses per 225 cycles vs 321; bank conflicts
    between lda/ldb/st and with the A/B DMA writes). Signature in g11pa: cyc/call above the static busy region
    by ~25-45 cycles; gA/call and gKB/ks at static.
 H2 DMA-starved A ring (core wins bank arbitration, S2MM throttled -> lock waits). Signature: cyc/call at static,
    gA/call (inside k-steps) >= static + 20.
 H3 B fifo starved (shim/memtile B broadcast to 8 columns). Signature: gKB/ks (k-step starts) >> static.
 H4 none of the above in the GEMM-only g11 (then the loss is a full-step interaction: EW traffic on the shared
    shim channel 0, dependency/finish timing).
